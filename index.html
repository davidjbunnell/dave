Welcome

What if one turn of the page could sweep you into danger, surprise, and characters you will not forget?

Welcome to the world of Dave Bunnell. The Winds pulls ordinary lives toward an extraordinary mystery. Body Run drags you into the shadows of a working mortuary, where a killer leaves his own signature behind. Touchdown Jesus looks like a football story, but underneath it is about standing up to a bully and refusing to become who everyone else expects.

Three books, three very different rides. Pick the one calling to you and see where the next page leads.

Thanks for stopping by, and happy reading. From, Dave Bunnell

About David

He Writes From a Life Fully Lived

Before he ever wrote a word of fiction, David J. Bunnell spent nearly four years working inside a fully operational mortuary. Today he is the author of three novels, each one drawn from a chapter of a life that reads stranger than fiction.
