Coming-of-Age / Football

Touchdown Jesus

Life does not come with a playbook.

Crem is the new kid, again. A new town, a new team, and the same old pressure to become whoever everyone else expects him to be. Beneath the Friday night lights, Touchdown Jesus is a story about bullying, family tension, and the quiet courage it takes to stand up for yourself when starting over feels impossible. It looks like a football story. Underneath, it is about refusing to let anyone else write your story for you.

Behind the Book

Touchdown Jesus is rooted in David's own real experiences of being bullied and starting over in a new town as a young athlete. The emotional weight of the novel, the isolation, the family tension, the pressure to prove yourself, comes directly from his life, not just his imagination.

What Readers Are Saying

So many sports books focus purely on the games, but Touchdown Jesus shines brightest in its quiet moments. It perfectly captures how isolating it feels to move during high school.

The emotional core of this book is incredibly grounded. When Crem drops the line, "guess that doesn't apply to dads," you can feel the immediate shift in the room's gravity. The author does a fantastic job showing how a parent's absence ripples through a kid's behavior.

The book masterfully balances the crushing weight of family tension with the thrilling hope of young love and a fresh start. If you love coming-of-age dramas, this is a must-read.
